Encoding formats

Invariant: funct7[31:25], funct3[14:12], and opcode[6:0] remain independent fields. Every instruction uses funct3=000.

R3 occupies funct7=0x00..0x50 and funct7=0x54..0x60. R2 uses bank 0x51 with xfunct5=0..31 except reserved value 0x09, then continues in bank 0x52. R1 uses bank 0x53 with xfunct10=0..3 and xfunct10=64..79. The fixed, no-operand ame.release encoding shares the R2 funct7=0x52 bank, using xfunct5=0x0a with rs1=rd=0; every other encoding under that selector is reserved. The 31 funct7 values 0x61..0x7f remain unallocated.
