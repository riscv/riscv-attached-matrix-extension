[discrete]
=== Provisional encoding registry

The encodings are provisional until the extension receives a stable RISC-V
allocation. The registry assigns a unique decode value to every AME
instruction.

All AME instructions retain the standard R-type selector skeleton:
`funct7[31:25]`, `funct3[14:12]`, and `opcode[6:0]`.  A format with fewer
register operands converts only the missing operand slots into extension
function space; it does not merge or relocate the selector fields.

[cols="1,2,2,1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
| Format | [31:25] | [24:20] | [19:15] | [14:12] | [11:7]

| R3 | `funct7` | `src2` | `src1` | `funct3=000` | `dest`
| R2 | `funct7` | `xfunct5` | `src1` | `funct3=000` | `dest`
| R1 | `funct7` | `xfunct10[9:5]` | `xfunct10[4:0]` | `funct3=000` | `dest`
|===

`opcode[6:0]` is `CUSTOM-1` (`0x2b`) and `funct3[14:12]` is `000` for every
AME instruction. R2 adds 5 selector bits and R1 adds 10 without changing the
locations or meanings of `funct7`, `funct3`, or `opcode`.

One `(funct7, funct3, opcode)` bank is assigned to one register format. A fixed
no-operand instruction may use a reserved selector in that bank when all other
encodings under that selector are reserved.

`ame.release` is the fixed member of the Resource Management R2 bank. It uses
`xfunct5=0x0A` with `rd=0` and `rs1=0`; every other encoding under that
selector is reserved. This does not define an additional register format.

`ame.acquire`, `ame.save`, and `ame.restore` take two source registers and no destination.
They therefore use the R3 skeleton with `dest[11:7]` fixed to zero and reserved, as `mrowunzip.ew` does.

[IMPORTANT]
====
The encoding of `ame.acquire` changed with the adoption of the context management model of <<shared-ame-backend>>: it is now an R3 encoding taking `rs1` and `rs2`, where it previously was an R2 encoding taking `rd` and `rs1`.
Any decoder written against an earlier draft must be updated.
The three `funct7` values allocated here for `ame.acquire`, `ame.restore`, and `ame.save` are provisional and are subject to confirmation with the opcode-map owner.
====

Within each register format, operation numbers follow the order of the encoded instructions in the category lists at the beginning of this chapter.
Instructions added after an allocation has been published extend it at the next free value rather than renumbering the existing encodings.
The generated `INSTRUCTION_ENCODINGS.md` file contains the complete `funct7`, `xfunct`, mask, and match values for those instructions.
